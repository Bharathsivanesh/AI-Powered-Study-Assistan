AI-Powered Study Assistant Knowledge Base

1. Dashboard:
- Teachers and staff can access the main dashboard after login.
- Dashboard shows analytics, notifications, and summary of uploaded courses, questions, and answers.

2. Generate Questions:
- Teachers can create a course and upload syllabus PDFs.
- AI generates questions based on the uploaded syllabus.
- Questions can be downloaded topic-wise or as a complete PDF.
- This section is for uploading syllabus and generating study questions for students.

3. Solve with AI:
- Teachers can upload question papers.
- AI generates answers automatically.
- Generated answers can be downloaded as PDFs.
- Teachers can upload these question-answer PDFs to courses, and students can access them via the app.

4. AI Chat Assistant:
- Staff and students can interact with the AI assistant to clarify doubts.
- The AI assistant provides guidance based on the platform’s features, e.g., how to create a course, upload syllabus, or check analytics.

5. AI Recommendation:
- Upload multiple question papers to analyze student performance and topic coverage.
- AI generates charts showing most repeated topics and recommendations for teachers and students.
- Analytics helps teachers identify important topics and improve study material.

6. Profile:
- Teachers and staff can update their profile information.
- Profile includes staff details, courses created, and uploaded content.
- Students can view teacher details and course info.

7. Notifications:
- Students sending doubts triggers notifications to teachers.
- Teachers replying triggers notifications to students.
- Notifications are visible on the top bar of the dashboard.

8. Sample FAQs:
- Q: How do I create a course?
  A: Go to Profile section there create course and  → Generate Questions → Upload syllabus PDF → AI generates questions → Save course. Students can access the uploaded questions and answers in their app.

- Q: How do I generate AI answers for a question paper?
  A: Go to Solve with AI → Upload your question paper → AI generates answers → Download the answer PDF → Add it to the course.

- Q: How can I get analytics on topics?
  A: Go to AI Recommendation → Upload multiple question papers → AI generates charts with most repeated topics and suggested focus areas.

- Q: How can a student clarify doubts?
  A: Students can chat with AI Chat Assistant or submit doubts → Notifications go to teachers → Teacher replies → Students get notifications.

- Q: Where can I check staff or course details?
  A: Go to Profile → View your profile and all uploaded courses and content.
